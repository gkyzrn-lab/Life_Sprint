## 📚 Added Majors (16 Total)

### STEM Fields

#### **Computer Science** (cs)
- **Difficulty**: High
- **GPA Required**: 3.3+
- **Job Outlook**: Excellent
- **Salaries**: $80K-$150K entry level
- **Challenge**: High demand but demanding coursework; intense competition; work-life balance in high-pressure roles
- **Career Path**: Tech careers, startups, innovation roles

#### **Engineering** (engineering)
- **Difficulty**: Very High
- **GPA Required**: 3.4+
- **Job Outlook**: Strong
- **Salaries**: $65K-$110K entry level
- **Challenge**: Rigorous coursework; licensing requirements (PE exam); geographically demanding fieldwork
- **Career Path**: Infrastructure, transportation, energy sectors

#### **Biology / Life Sciences** (biology)
- **Difficulty**: High
- **GPA Required**: 3.4+
- **Job Outlook**: Moderate
- **Salaries**: $40K-$70K entry level (Lab/Research Tech)
- **Challenge**: Lab-dependent work; limited direct employment without specialization; requires advanced degrees
- **Career Path**: Medicine, research, environmental roles

#### **Mathematics** (mathematics)
- **Difficulty**: Very High
- **GPA Required**: 3.5+
- **Job Outlook**: Moderate
- **Salaries**: $55K-$95K (with specialization)
- **Challenge**: Abstract thinking intensive; limited direct employment; competition with specialized STEM fields
- **Career Path**: Finance, cryptography, AI, research

#### **Data Science / Analytics** (data_science)
- **Difficulty**: Very High
- **GPA Required**: 3.4+
- **Job Outlook**: Excellent
- **Salaries**: $75K-$130K entry level
- **Challenge**: Rapidly changing tools/methods; need for both business and technical skills; constant learning
- **Career Path**: Tech, consulting, finance, any data-driven industry

### Social Sciences

#### **Psychology** (psychology)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **GPA Required**: 3.2+
- **Job Outlook**: Moderate
- **Salaries**: $45K-$65K entry level (without license)
- **Challenge**: Licensing requirements (PhD/Masters); emotionally demanding; student debt for grad school; competitive programs
- **Career Path**: Clinical work, HR, UX research, counseling, academia

#### **Economics** (economics)
- **Difficulty**: High
- **GPA Required**: 3.3+
- **Job Outlook**: Strong
- **Salaries**: $60K-$100K entry level
- **Challenge**: Heavy math/statistics; methodology debates; need for strong technical skills; policy navigation
- **Career Path**: Policy, finance, consulting, research, central banks

#### **Political Science** (politics)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **GPA Required**: 3.1+
- **Job Outlook**: Moderate
- **Salaries**: $45K-$75K entry level
- **Challenge**: Lower salaries than private sector; requires reputation building; political climate affects job availability
- **Career Path**: Public service, law, diplomacy, advocacy, research

#### **History** (history)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **GPA Required**: 3.0+
- **Job Outlook**: Limited
- **Salaries**: $40K-$60K entry level
- **Challenge**: Highly competitive academia; requires advanced degrees; declining teaching positions; low history-specific salaries
- **Career Path**: Academia, museums, cultural institutions, law, journalism, policy

#### **Sociology** (sociology)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **GPA Required**: 3.0+
- **Job Outlook**: Moderate
- **Salaries**: $45K-$65K entry level
- **Challenge**: Research-dependent funding; requires portfolio building; field work can be challenging
- **Career Path**: Policy, nonprofits, community work, corporate research

### Business & Economics

#### **Business Administration** (ba) - *Previously Minimal*
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **GPA Required**: 3.0+
- **Job Outlook**: Strong
- **Salaries**: $50K-$85K entry level
- **Challenge**: Competitive job market; constant need for certifications (MBA, CPA); work culture demanding
- **Career Path**: Management, consulting, finance, operations, entrepreneurship

#### **Accounting** (accounting) - *NEW*
- **Difficulty**: High
- **GPA Required**: 3.2+
- **Job Outlook**: Excellent
- **Salaries**: $50K-$75K entry level ($75K-$120K+ with CPA)

#### **Finance** (finance) - *NEW*
- **Difficulty**: High
- **GPA Required**: 3.3+
- **Job Outlook**: Strong
- **Salaries**: $70K-$150K+ (varies by firm)
- **Challenge**: Market volatility; intense banking hours; high-pressure decisions; licensing requirements
- **Career Path**: Investment banking, corporate finance, trading, risk management

### Humanities

#### **Communications / Media** (communications) - *NEW*

- **Job Outlook**: Changing

### Healthcare

#### **Nursing** (nursing) - *NEW*
- **Difficulty**: High
- **GPA Required**: 3.2+
- **Job Outlook**: Excellent

## 🎮 Real-World Educational Value

### What Players Learn

1. **Realistic Career Paths**: Each major maps to real jobs with authentic salaries, hours, and stress levels
2. **Trade-offs**: Players see that high salary often means high stress or difficult prerequisites
3. **Specialization**: Some majors require advanced degrees (nursing, accounting, psychology) - real-world requirement
4. **Job Market Reality**: Limited job outlooks (history) vs excellent (data science) reflect actual market
5. **Challenge Difficulty**: Major difficulty ratings help players choose appropriate challenge levels
6. **Interdisciplinary Skills**: Jobs don't always match majors perfectly (psychology → UX research, history → law, etc.)

### Educational Topics

**Majors Include Context On:**
- Required certifications (CPA, PE, RN, LCSW)
- Graduate school needs (Psychology, Engineering, History, Academic paths)
- Licensing requirements (Nursing, Accounting)
- Industry disruption (Communications, Journalism)
- Job market competitiveness (CS, Finance vs English, History)
- Emerging fields (Data Science with high demand)
- Work culture realities (Investment Banking brutal hours, Research funding uncertainty)
