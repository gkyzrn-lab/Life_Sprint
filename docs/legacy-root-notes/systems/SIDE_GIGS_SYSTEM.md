# Side Gigs & Freelancing System

## 📖 Overview

The **Side Gigs & Freelancing System** teaches teens about **modern income opportunities beyond traditional employment**. This system reflects the reality of Gen Z's workforce: the gig economy provides flexibility but income varies, passive income takes time (6-12 months), entrepreneurship is risky (80% failure rate), and skills directly translate to earning power.

### Key Educational Goals

1. **Gig Economy Reality**: Flexible hours but variable income ($12-150/hr range)
2. **Passive Income Patience**: Takes 6-12 months before meaningful earnings
3. **Entrepreneurship Risk**: High failure rates (1-50% success) but high upside
4. **Skill Monetization**: Technical skills can earn 5-10x more than low-skill work
5. **Income Diversification**: Multiple streams reduce financial vulnerability
6. **Time Management**: Balance between income and burnout (60+ hrs/week warning)
7. **Portfolio Building**: Initial low rates build portfolio for future high rates
8. **Capital Requirements**: Businesses need $500-$3000 startup investment

## 🎯 Portfolio & Skill System

### Portfolio Quality (0-100)

**What It Is**: Measure of your freelance work quality and experience.

**How It Grows**:
- **+0.5 per hour** of freelance work with `portfolio_boost=True`
- Freelance writing, design, coding, photography all build portfolio
- Delivery/rideshare jobs do NOT build portfolio

**Thresholds**:
- **0-49**: Beginner freelancer, limited opportunities
- **50**: "Portfolio Builder" achievement unlocked
- **60**: Unlocks experienced freelance writing ($40-100/hr)
- **70**: Unlocks experienced graphic design ($50-125/hr)
- **75**: Unlocks experienced coding ($70-150/hr)
- **100**: Master freelancer, premium rates

**Teaching Moment**: "Your early freelance work is an INVESTMENT. Low pay now = portfolio that unlocks $100/hr rates later."

**Lesson**: Always calculate NET income after expenses.

**Lesson**: Delayed gratification. Compound growth over time.

**Reality**: $50 article = $12.50/hr initially, but builds to $100/hr rates.  
**Lesson**: Low initial rates are investment in future earning power.

### 5. Business Capital & Risk
**Reality**: $500 startup, 10% success rate, 6 months to breakeven.  
**Lesson**: Entrepreneurship requires capital and carries high risk.

### 6. Skill = Income
**Reality**: Delivery $18/hr vs coding $100/hr (5-10x difference).  
**Lesson**: Invest in high-value skills (coding, design, writing).

### 7. Diversification Security
**Reality**: One stream drops 50%, total income only drops 25%.  
**Lesson**: Multiple income sources = financial resilience.

### 8. Time Management
**Reality**: 118 hours scheduled + 56 sleep = 174 > 168 hours/week.  
**Lesson**: Can't do everything. Burnout is real. Prioritize.
