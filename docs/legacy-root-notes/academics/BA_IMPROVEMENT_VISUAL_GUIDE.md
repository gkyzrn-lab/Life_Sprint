   - Higher completion rates

   - Learn by doing, not reading
   - Solve real problems

   - Easy → Medium → Hard
   - Build confidence
   - Increase difficulty gradually

## Examples of Real Scenarios

### BA102: New Business Simulation
```
Starting scenario: You invest $50K personal savings
Question 1: What happens to your balance sheet?
  → Students CALCULATE, not guess

Mid-scenario: Bank gives you $30K loan
Question 2: Now what's your equity?
  → Students understand debt ≠ equity

End scenario: You earn $15K revenue (collect $10K, owed $5K)
Question 3: How to record?
  → Students understand accrual accounting

RESULT: Student built accounting from scratch!
```

### BA202: Cash Flow Scenario
```
Month 1: Sell $100K (80% cash, 20% credit)
Month 2: Credit sales from Month 1 collected
Question: How much cash Month 2?

INSIGHT: Profitable months lag behind cash months!
LEARNING: Accounts receivable creates timing gaps
REAL WORLD: Why Amazon collects fast (Amazon Prime)
            why B2B companies need working capital
```

### BA301: Org Design
```
Scenario: Growing from 10 → 50 employees
Question: How many managers do you need?

Students learn:
  - Span of control principle (3-6 per manager)
  - Need middle management at scale
  - Flat vs hierarchical trade-offs

RESULT: Understands why organizations are structured like they are
```
